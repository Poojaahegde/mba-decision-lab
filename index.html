04 / Method

How to read this model

01

Break-even

Fixed monthly cost ÷ (price − variable cost per unit), rounded up. If contribution per unit is zero or less, break-even is unavailable.

02

Cash flow

Each month: units × (price − variable cost) − fixed cost. Starting cash flow equals negative upfront investment. Sales volume compounds at the entered monthly growth rate.

03

Present value

NPV sums monthly profit discounted using an effective monthly rate derived from the annual rate, then subtracts upfront investment. A positive NPV depends entirely on the assumptions.

Scope: simplified operating model. It excludes tax, debt, working capital, capacity limits, seasonality, and terminal value. Use verified inputs for a real decision.
